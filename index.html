Privacy Policy Overview

KrewsUp Technologies Private Limited operates the KrewsUp mobile application to connect event organizers with verified event staff through our secure digital platform.

This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

Who Can Use KrewsUp

Event Organizers

  • Event management companies
  • Corporate companies organizing internal/external events
  • Multinational corporations (MNCs) and large enterprises
  • Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs)
  • Corporate event planners and HR departments
  • Individual event organizers
  • Wedding planners and organizers
  • Government organizations and public sector units
  • Educational institutions (universities, colleges, schools)
  • Non-profit organizations and NGOs
  • Hotels and hospitality businesses
  • Must be 18 years or older to register

Event Staff (Gig Workers)

  • Verified professionals seeking event work opportunities
  • Freelancers in the event industry
  • Part-time workers looking for flexible employment
  • Must be 18 years or older and complete our KYC verification process

Eligibility Requirements

  • Users must be at least 18 years of age
  • Users must provide accurate and complete information during registration
  • Event staff must successfully complete our verification process
  • Users must comply with all applicable laws and regulations

What We Collect

  • Full name and contact information (email address, phone number)
  • Profile photograph
  • Location data (city, state)
  • Account credentials (username, password)
  • Payment information (bank details, UPI ID, digital wallet information)
  • Device information (device type, operating system, unique device identifiers)
  • Company name and business details
  • Company registration documents and GST details (for corporate clients)
  • Authorized representative information
  • Event history and preferences
  • Corporate event requirements and specifications
  • Budget and procurement policies
  • Compliance and vendor management requirements
  • Feedback and ratings provided to event staff
  • Government-issued identification documents (for KYC verification)
  • Professional qualifications and certifications
  • Work experience and portfolio
  • Skill sets and specializations
  • Bank account details for payment processing
  • Feedback and ratings received from event organizers
  • Work availability and scheduling preferences
  • Usage data (how you interact with our app)
  • Location information (with your consent)
  • Device information and identifiers
  • Log data (IP address, browser type, pages visited)
  • Analytics data to improve our services
  • Social media platforms (if you choose to connect your accounts)
  • Payment processors
  • Identity verification services
  • Background check providers (for enhanced verification)

Communication

  • Comply with legal obligations and regulatory requirements

  • Enforce our Terms of Service

Data Visibility and Sharing

Information Sharing Between Users

Event Organizers can see:

  • Event staff profiles (name, photo, skills, ratings, experience)
  • Verified status and certifications
  • Availability and location (general area, not precise address)
  • Past work history and feedback from other organizers

Event Staff can see:

  • Event organizer profiles (company name, ratings, event history)
  • Event details and requirements
  • Compensation and payment terms
  • Event location and timing

Public Information

  • Profile pictures and names
  • Professional skills and qualifications
  • Ratings and reviews (anonymized)
  • General location (city/area, not specific address)

Third-Party Sharing

Service Providers

  • Payment processors for transaction handling
  • Identity verification services for KYC compliance
  • Cloud storage providers for data hosting
  • Analytics services for platform improvement
  • Communication services for messaging and notifications

Legal Requirements

  • Government authorities when required by law
  • Law enforcement agencies for investigation purposes
  • Legal advisors for compliance and dispute resolution

Business Transfers

  • In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of our business
  • With explicit user consent and proper notification

What We Do Not Share

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

Safety Measures

Technical Safeguards

  • End-to-end encryption for sensitive data transmission
  • Secure Socket Layer (SSL) technology for data protection
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Multi-factor authentication for account access
  • Secure data storage with encrypted databases

Operational Safeguards

  • Limited access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular employee training on data protection
  • Incident response procedures for security breaches
  • Regular backup and disaster recovery processes

Physical Safeguards

  • Secure data centers with restricted access
  • Environmental controls and monitoring systems
  • Proper disposal of physical media containing personal data

Verification and Trust

  • Government ID verification for all event staff
  • Background verification for enhanced security
  • Skill assessment and certification validation
  • Continuous monitoring and re-verification

Payment Security

  • PCI DSS compliant payment processing
  • Encrypted storage of payment information
  • Secure payment gateways and fraud detection
  • Regular transaction monitoring

Your Rights and Control

Account Management

  • Access your personal information stored on our platform
  • Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Download your data in a portable format
  • Restrict processing of your personal information

Privacy Settings

  • Visibility of your profile information
  • Communication preferences and notifications
  • Location sharing settings
  • Data sharing with third-party services

Data Retention

  • Account data: Deleted within 30 days of account closure
  • Transaction records: Retained for 7 years for tax and legal compliance
  • Communication logs: Retained for 2 years for dispute resolution

Consent Management

  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Disable location tracking
  • Revoke permissions for third-party integrations

Exercising Your Rights

  1. Contact us through the provided channels
  2. Verify your identity for security purposes
  3. Specify the nature of your request
  4. We will respond within 30 days of receiving your request

Children's Privacy

KrewsUp does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 years of age. If we discover that we have collected personal information from a child under 18, we will delete such information immediately. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.
